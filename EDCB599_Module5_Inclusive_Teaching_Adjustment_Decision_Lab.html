How to use the lab

Complete each challenge after working through the module content on differentiation, reasonable adjustments, consultation, cultural responsiveness and EAL/D support. Focus on the intended learning first, then the barrier, consultation, support and review.

Core principle: inclusion does not mean lowering expectations. Maintain the learning, remove unnecessary barriers, consult appropriately, provide targeted support and review whether the response is effective.
Challenge 1

Identify the barrier

🔎

Choose the barrier that is most clearly supported by the available evidence. Avoid making assumptions from labels, diagnoses or backgrounds alone.

Challenge 2

Adjustment, support or modification?

🧩

Sort each example according to what the teacher is changing. Drag cards, or tap a card and then tap a destination.

Reasonable adjustment

Changes access or participation while preserving the intended learning.

Additional teaching / scaffolding

Provides instruction or support so the student can access the same learning.

Change to conditions

Changes timing, setting or mode without necessarily changing the construct.

Changes the assessed learning

Alters or removes the knowledge or skill being assessed.

Challenge 5

Keep the challenge, remove the barrier

🛠️

Select the response that best preserves the intellectual demand of the Business task while making the learning accessible.

Business task

Students must analyse a short business case and write an evidence-based recommendation.

Student evidence

An EAL/D student demonstrates strong conceptual understanding in discussion but is still developing the academic English needed to organise an extended written response.

The intended learning is Business reasoning and communication, not simply producing a fixed number of words without support.
