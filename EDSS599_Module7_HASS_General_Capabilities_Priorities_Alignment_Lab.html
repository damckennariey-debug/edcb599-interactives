Alignment lens

General Capabilities and Cross-curriculum Priorities should not be added after planning is complete. They are most meaningful when they help students engage more deeply with substantive HASS knowledge, disciplinary concepts and inquiry.

Core principle: capabilities and priorities enrich HASS when they strengthen the disciplinary learning.

Challenge 4

Cross-curriculum Priority authenticity check

🧭

Sort each example into the category that best describes the way the priority has been used. Drag cards, or tap a card and then tap a destination.

Authentic integration

The priority strengthens the intended HASS learning in a respectful, substantive way.

Tokenistic inclusion

The priority appears as decoration or an add-on without strengthening the learning.

Stereotypical / problematic

The example oversimplifies, stereotypes or uses an inappropriate representation.

Not actually relevant

The priority does not meaningfully connect to the intended learning.
