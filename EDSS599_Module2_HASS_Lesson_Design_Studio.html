Design lens

An effective HASS lesson is not simply a collection of interesting activities. Strong planning aligns curriculum, learning objectives, teaching moves, student practice and evidence of learning while taking account of prior knowledge and learner needs.

Core principle: start with what students need to learn and what evidence will show it — then select the sequence, explanations, modelling, practice and activities that support that learning.
