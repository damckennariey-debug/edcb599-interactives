Pedagogy Decision Lab

Practise selecting, combining and adapting pedagogical approaches for Year 7–10 Business and Economics. The challenge is not to choose the “most engaging” strategy, but to choose the approach that best supports the intended learning for these students at this point in their learning.

Overall mastery
0 / 4 challenges complete

How to use the lab

Complete each challenge after working through the module content on guided inquiry, explicit instruction, collaborative learning and disciplinary literacy. Incorrect attempts are useful: read the feedback and reconsider the instructional purpose, the students’ prior knowledge and the amount of guidance required.

Core principle: student activity does not necessarily demonstrate student learning. Effective pedagogy is purposeful, responsive and aligned with curriculum intent.
Challenge 1

Choose the approach

🧭

Select the strongest primary approach for each scenario. In some cases, the best answer is a deliberate combination of approaches.

Challenge 2

How much guidance?

🎚️

Sort each classroom situation according to the level of teacher guidance most appropriate at that point. Drag cards, or tap a card and then tap a destination.

High teacher guidance

New, complex or unfamiliar learning; substantial modelling and scaffolding required.

Shared responsibility

Students have some knowledge; teacher prompts, guides and checks while students increasingly contribute.

Greater independence

Students have demonstrated readiness to apply knowledge or skills with reduced support.
