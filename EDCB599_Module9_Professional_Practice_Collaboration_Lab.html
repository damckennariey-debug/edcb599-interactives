Professional practice lens

Teaching is collaborative. Beginning Business teachers work with colleagues, support staff, leaders, families, professional bodies and wider networks. The aim is not to collect contacts or attend random professional development, but to use the right source of support for the right professional need.

Core principle: identify the professional need, choose credible and relevant support, apply what you learn, gather evidence and refine your practice.
