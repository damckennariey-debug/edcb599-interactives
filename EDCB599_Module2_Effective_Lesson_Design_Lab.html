Effective Lesson Design Lab

Analyse, reconstruct and improve Year 7–10 Business and Economics lesson design. The focus is not on how many activities a lesson contains, but on whether curriculum, objectives, sequencing, explanation, practice, assessment and responsiveness work together to support learning.

0 / 4 challenges complete

How to use the lab

Complete each challenge before returning to the sample lesson plans and Decision Making Matrix. The scenarios are designed to help you practise professional judgement about lesson quality.

Key idea: an engaging lesson is not automatically an effective lesson. Look for alignment, manageable sequencing, explicit explanation and modelling, opportunities for practice, evidence of learning, feedback and responsiveness to students.

Challenge 4

Respond to evidence

🧠

A Year 9 Business class has just completed a short formative check on competitive advantage. Choose the teacher response that best uses the evidence to guide the next step.

Exit-ticket evidence

  • 8 students accurately explain competitive advantage and apply it to a new business example.
  • 14 students can define the term but give weak or generic examples.
  • 6 students confuse competitive advantage with simply being “the biggest business”.

The next lesson is scheduled to move into how businesses create and maintain competitive advantage.
