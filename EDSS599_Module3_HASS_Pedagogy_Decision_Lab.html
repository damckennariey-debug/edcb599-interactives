Decision lens

Explicit instruction, inquiry learning and collaborative learning are not competing “brands” of teaching. Effective HASS teachers select and combine approaches according to what students are learning, what they already know, the complexity of the task and the amount of guidance required.

Core principle: pedagogy follows the learning problem. Student activity alone is not evidence of student learning.

🧠

Challenge 2

How much guidance?

🎚️

Classify each scenario according to the level of guidance students most likely need at that point in learning.

High guidance

Clear explanation, modelling, worked examples and close support.

Guided practice

Students practise with prompts, feedback and teacher support.

Partial scaffold

Students have more responsibility but still use structured supports.

Independent inquiry

Students apply established knowledge and skills more independently.

Challenge 4

I do → We do → You do

🪜

Sort each teaching move into the most appropriate phase of an explicit-instruction sequence.

I do

Teacher explanation, modelling and think-aloud.

We do

Guided co-construction and supported practice.

You do

Independent application using a fresh example.

Not yet

Moves that would be premature before knowledge/skill is established.
