Planning lens

Strong HASS planning begins with what students need to learn, the prior knowledge and skills they require, and the evidence that will show success. Source analysis is not simply something students are told to “do”; unfamiliar disciplinary reasoning needs to be explained, modelled, practised and gradually released.

Core principle: source analysis is taught, not assigned.
