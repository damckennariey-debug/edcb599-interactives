Your curriculum navigation brief

Use this activity before completing the module’s curriculum mapping and self-audit tasks. The aim is to organise your HASS curriculum knowledge so that you can locate the right documents, recognise how they are structured and identify where further professional learning may be needed.

Core principle: curriculum familiarity is not the same as curriculum understanding. Strong curriculum knowledge means being able to explain what students need to learn, how that learning progresses and how achievement is demonstrated.
