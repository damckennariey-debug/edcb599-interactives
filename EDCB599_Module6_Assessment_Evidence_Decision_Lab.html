Assessment & Evidence Decision Lab

Practise deciding why assessment is being used, what the evidence can actually support, how to design valid assessment, how moderation should work and what makes feedback actionable.

How to use the lab

Complete each challenge after working through the module content. Focus on the relationship between intended learning, evidence and instructional response.

Core principle: assessment improves learning only when the evidence gathered is sufficiently valid, interpreted carefully and used to inform an appropriate response.
Challenge 1

What is the assessment purpose?

🎯

Judge the purpose from how the evidence will be used, not from the format of the activity.

Challenge 2

What does the evidence actually tell you?

📊

Choose the most defensible interpretation. Avoid claims that exceed the quality of the evidence.

Challenge 3

Design a valid assessment

🧪

Select the item that best elicits the intended knowledge or skill.

Challenge 4

Moderation room

⚖️

Two teachers have judged the same Business response differently. What should happen next?

Teacher A

Judges the response as B-standard because the student uses relevant evidence and explains the likely effect of the strategy on the target market.

Teacher B

Judges the response as C-standard because the explanation is brief and one piece of evidence is only partially integrated.

Challenge 5

Make the feedback actionable

💬

Choose the feedback most likely to help the student improve the intended Business reasoning.
