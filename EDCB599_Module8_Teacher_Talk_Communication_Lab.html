Communication lens

Effective classroom communication helps students understand what they are learning, what they are expected to do and how they can improve. The goal is not simply more teacher talk or more student talk, but communication that supports attention, understanding, practice and application.

Core principle: reduce ambiguity, make thinking visible, check understanding and use participation structures that support broad, respectful engagement.
