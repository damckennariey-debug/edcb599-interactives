Planning lens

Begin with the intended Business and Economics learning. Then identify which capabilities or priorities genuinely support that learning, what needs to be explicitly taught or scaffolded, what students will do, and what evidence will show development.

Core principle: start with the Business learning, select only what genuinely aligns, teach the necessary knowledge and processes explicitly, and gather evidence that students actually developed them.
