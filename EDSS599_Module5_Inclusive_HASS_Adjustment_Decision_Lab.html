Responsive teaching lens

Inclusive HASS teaching is proactive. It begins by clarifying the intended learning, identifying the barrier, consulting appropriately, and selecting supports or adjustments that improve access without unnecessarily lowering the curriculum expectation.

Core principle: keep the challenge; remove the barrier.

Sort each example by its primary function. Drag cards, or tap a card and then tap a destination.

Whole-class differentiation

Evidence-informed teaching designed to improve access and learning for many students.

Additional teaching / support

Targeted scaffolding, feedback or teaching while maintaining the same intended learning.

Reasonable adjustment

A change enabling a student with disability to access or demonstrate learning on the same basis as peers.

Curriculum / assessment modification

A change to the knowledge, skill or construct being taught or assessed.
