Communication lens

Teacher communication is part of pedagogy and classroom management. Explanations, modelling, questioning, instructions, feedback and discussion structures help students understand what they are learning, how HASS reasoning works, what is expected and how to improve.

Core principle: teacher talk should make the learning clearer — not simply make the teacher talk more.
